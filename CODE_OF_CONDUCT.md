# Code of Conduct

AI Werewolf aims to maintain a focused, respectful, and safe environment for contributors and users.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their technical merits.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect different experience levels, identities, and communication styles.
- Protect private information and remove sensitive data from reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Publishing another person's private information
- Sexualized, violent, or otherwise inappropriate conduct directed at participants
- Spam, coordinated disruption, or deliberate misuse of project services
- Pressuring contributors to disclose credentials, account data, or private communications

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or the private security-advisory channel when the report also involves a security or privacy issue. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary.
